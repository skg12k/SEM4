Institutional Blindspots

A Corporate Post-Mortem on the Securities Scam of 1992 — Analyzing Systemic Failures, Behavioral Pressures, and Regulatory Evolution.
1

Market Context & Behavior

BSE Sensex Trajectory (1991 - 1992)
15 Days
Opportunity Gap: A 15-day manual clearing window for Bank Receipts allowed fake documents to circulate without cross-verification.
4x Surge
Market Pressure: Sustaining the "Big Bull" persona required continuous massive liquidity inflows to prop up artificially inflated equity prices.
2

The Principal-Agent Failure

Brokers exploited institutional silos, acting as unauthorized shadow counterparties and monopolizing information between banks.

Principal
Banks seeking anonymity in trades
Appointed
Agent
Brokers executing bond transactions
Diverted to
Market
High-risk leveraged equity bets
Management Lesson: Misaligned incentives destroy wealth. Banking operations must be strictly separated from speculative trading.
3

Systemic Breakdown: The Swiss Cheese Model

Layer 01
Internal Bank Controls
Mid-level management bypassed physical verification of government bonds before issuing BRs.
Layer 02
Inter-Bank Comms
Institutions blindly trusted brokers as intermediaries, forgoing direct counterparty verification.
Layer 03
The Central Bank
Sluggish manual monitoring failed to flag massive anomalies and duplicate transactions.
Layer 04
The Watchdog
Nascent regulatory authority lacked statutory power and punitive teeth at the time.
Systemic Breach
Four independent governance layers failed simultaneously, enabling ₹4,000 Crores to be illegally siphoned.
4

The Regulatory Legacy

From manual trust-based networks to algorithmic verification. The regulatory watershed outcomes:

NSE Established

Electronic screen-based trading replaced opaque, relationship-driven trading floor mechanisms.

Depositories Act

Dematerialization of shares eliminated the physical securities forgery that enabled the scam.

SEBI Empowered

The regulator gained statutory authority to investigate and rigorously prosecute market participants.
